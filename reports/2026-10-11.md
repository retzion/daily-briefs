## Featured: Claude Dashboards (Beta)

Claude Dashboards, launched October 8 in beta on paid plans, lets you build live, auto-updating dashboards from plain-language questions — no SQL required.

**What it is:** You describe what you want to see ("show me weekly active users by region for the last 90 days"), and Claude generates a live dashboard connected to your data warehouse. Supported connectors at launch: Amazon Redshift, BigQuery, ClickHouse, Databricks, Snowflake, plus existing connectors like Salesforce.

**Why it's useful:** It collapses the feedback loop between "I have a question about our data" and "I can see the answer." No waiting for an analyst to write a query or build a chart — ask, get a dashboard, share it. Dashboards stay live and update as underlying data changes.

**Quick example:**
1. Open Claude → select Dashboards
2. Connect your BigQuery instance (one-time setup)
3. Ask: "Compare conversion rates by acquisition channel, last 30 days vs previous 30 days"
4. Claude generates the dashboard with charts and tables — share the link with your team

Dashboards are off by default for Enterprise orgs (admin opt-in). Claude Motion, announced alongside it, generates animated MP4 explainer videos from prompts using code-based animation rather than video generation models.
