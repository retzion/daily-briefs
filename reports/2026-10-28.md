## Featured: Claude Duet (Research Preview)

Claude Duet is Anthropic's new proactive agent, announced October 21. Unlike Claude's chat interface where you initiate every interaction, Duet monitors your connected apps in the background and reaches out when something needs your attention.

**What it does:**
- Sends a morning briefing summarizing what matters across your connected apps
- Monitors throughout the day and pings you only when a decision requires your input
- Handles routine tasks with mandatory approval gates — must show you a proposed action before sending messages, making payments, signing documents, or deleting content

**Access & limits:**
- US-only waitlist, Pro and Max subscribers, no extra cost
- iOS, Android, and claude.ai
- Built on Claude Opus 5.5
- Shares usage limits with other Claude products

**Why it matters:** Duet represents Anthropic's move into persistent, always-on agents that act on your behalf rather than waiting for prompts. Competes directly with OpenAI's Dots and Meta's Muse. The approval-gate design trades autonomy for trust — requiring human confirmation on consequential actions.
