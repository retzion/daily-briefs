## Featured: Microsoft 365 Connector Write Tools

New in Claude Code v2.1.219, the Microsoft 365 connector now supports **write operations** — not just reading your email and calendar, but acting on them.

**What you can do:**
- **Email**: Draft and send messages, reply to threads, manage folders
- **Calendar**: Create, update, and cancel events; check availability
- **OneDrive/SharePoint**: Create and update files, organize folders
- **Mailbox**: Update settings and rules

**Why it's useful**: Turns Claude Code into a productivity hub. Ask Claude to "draft a follow-up email to the team about the deployment" or "schedule a review meeting for Thursday at 2pm" without switching to Outlook. Particularly powerful in routines — a morning brief can check your calendar, summarize unread emails, and draft replies.

**Setup**: Connect Microsoft 365 from the Connectors panel in Claude Code on the web, or via `claude mcp login microsoft-365` in the CLI. Requires an M365 account with appropriate permissions.

**Example prompt:**
```
Check my calendar for tomorrow and draft an email to the team
summarizing the meetings I have scheduled.
```
