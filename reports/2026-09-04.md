## Featured: `/diff` (Live Diff Panel)

New in today's Claude Code release, `/diff` opens a side-by-side diff panel in fullscreen mode that shows your working tree's uncommitted changes updating live as Claude edits files. It gives you real-time visibility into what's changing without leaving the conversation.

**Why it's useful:** Previously, you had to `git diff` in a separate terminal or wait until Claude finished to review changes. The diff panel lets you watch edits as they happen — catch mistakes early, understand the scope of changes, and build confidence in what's being modified.

**Usage:**
```
# Enter fullscreen mode first
/tui fullscreen

# Toggle the diff panel on/off
/diff
```

The panel shows a standard unified diff of all uncommitted changes, refreshing as Claude makes edits. Combined with fullscreen mode's mouse support and zero-flicker rendering, it turns Claude Code into a more IDE-like experience.
