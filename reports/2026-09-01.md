## Featured: Artifact Watches (`action: "watch"`)

Artifact Watches let a Claude Code session subscribe to a published artifact so it gets woken up automatically when the artifact is republished by someone else or when a viewer sends a comment thread to Claude. This enables collaborative workflows where multiple people work on the same artifact without anyone manually polling for changes.

**Why it's useful:** If you publish a dashboard, report, or interactive page as an artifact and share it with your team, watches mean you don't have to keep checking for feedback. When a teammate leaves a comment and sends it to Claude, your session wakes up, reads the comment, and can act on it — updating the artifact, replying to the thread, or resolving it.

**Quick usage:**
```
# Publishing automatically arms a watch
Artifact({ file_path: "dashboard.html", favicon: "📊" })

# Manually watch an existing artifact
Artifact({ action: "watch", url: "https://claude.ai/code/artifact/abc123" })

# Check what you're watching
Artifact({ action: "status" })

# Stop watching
Artifact({ action: "unwatch", url: "https://claude.ai/code/artifact/abc123" })
```

Watches are durable server-side subscriptions — they survive across turns and don't require a live connection. When a wake arrives, re-read the artifact and its comments before acting.
