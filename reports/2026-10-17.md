## Featured: `/advisor` (Advisor Tool)
The `/advisor` slash command lets you assign a secondary model as a "second brain" for your primary coding model. When you invoke `/advisor`, a picker lets you choose an advisor model (e.g., Opus while coding with Sonnet). The advisor doesn't take actions — it reviews your current context and provides strategic guidance, alternative approaches, or catches issues the primary model might miss.

**Why it's useful:** When you're deep in a complex debugging session or architectural decision and the primary model is going in circles, the advisor offers a fresh perspective without losing your session context. It's particularly effective for code review within a session, sanity-checking plans before execution, and getting a second opinion on tricky logic.

**Quick usage:**
```
/advisor              # opens model picker
/advisor opus         # sets Opus as advisor directly
```

You can also set it persistently via `advisorModel` in settings or `--advisor` on the CLI. As of v2.1.300, `/advisor` is now available on Claude Platform on AWS in addition to the Anthropic API.

([Official docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/advisor)) ([makandracards guide](https://makandracards.com/makandra/626779-claude-code-advisor-tool))
