## Featured: Smart Reports (Enterprise Beta)
Smart Reports, launched September 10 on Claude Enterprise plans, analyze how a team uses Claude and surface actionable insights: what work is getting done, what it costs, where sessions hit friction, and which repeated patterns could be packaged as shared skills.

**Why it's useful:** Enterprise admins often lack visibility into how their teams actually use Claude — which projects consume the most tokens, which workflows stall, and what institutional knowledge keeps getting re-derived. Smart Reports close that gap automatically.

**How it works:**
- Navigate to the Admin panel in your Claude Enterprise workspace
- Select "Smart Reports" and generate a new report
- Claude analyzes usage across your organization and produces a structured breakdown covering adoption patterns, cost drivers, friction points, and skill recommendations
- Up to 10 free reports/month during beta

**Important:** Smart Reports are designed for planning and investment decisions — Anthropic explicitly states they should not be used for evaluating individual performance or making employment decisions.

Not available for orgs using CMEK, HIPAA configs, or zero data retention.
