## Featured: Artifact Pinning (`action: "pin"` / `action: "unpin"`)

When you publish artifacts from Claude Code — dashboards, trackers, reference pages, design canvases — they live at stable URLs you can share. But finding them again means digging through your artifact gallery or searching conversation history.

**Artifact Pinning** solves this by letting you pin frequently-used artifacts to your claude.ai sidebar for one-click access. Pinned artifacts appear persistently in the left sidebar, like bookmarks for your most important pages.

**How it works:**
- Claude can pin an artifact after publishing: `Artifact({ action: "pin", url: "https://claude.ai/artifacts/..." })`
- Or unpin: `Artifact({ action: "unpin", url: "https://..." })`
- You can also ask Claude: *"Pin that dashboard to my sidebar"*
- Pins are private to your account — they don't affect who can see the artifact
- From the CLI: `/artifacts` lists your artifacts; `ctrl+]` reopens the most recent one

**When it's useful:** Pin your team's daily standup tracker, a project dashboard you check hourly, or a reference page you keep open alongside code. Beats bookmarking URLs or scrolling through conversation history.
