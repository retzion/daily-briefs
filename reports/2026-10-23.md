## Featured: `anthropic-skills:morning` (Morning Brief Skill)

Claude Code ships with a built-in `anthropic-skills:morning` skill that generates a structured morning briefing for your day. Invoke it from any project to get a scannable summary of what needs your attention — recent changes, open issues, pending reviews, and priorities.

**Why it's useful:** Starting your day with `morning` surfaces the most important items across your project without manually checking Git logs, issue trackers, and PR queues. It's a lightweight alternative to building a custom daily routine and pairs well with Claude Code Routines for fully automated morning check-ins.

**Usage:**
```
# In Claude Code, just type:
/morning

# Or combine with a routine for an automated daily brief:
# Set up a scheduled routine that invokes the morning skill
# at your preferred start-of-day time
```

The skill examines your repo state, connected integrations, and recent activity to produce a prioritized brief. Combine it with Google Workspace or Slack integrations for a comprehensive daily overview that covers code, communications, and calendar.
